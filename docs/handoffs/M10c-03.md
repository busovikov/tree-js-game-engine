## Decisions and invariants

- `@haku/audio` remains DOM/Web-Audio-free; only `@haku/audio-web` owns browser audio
  objects, decoding, autoplay unlock, and AudioContext lifecycle.
- A real UI click must call `unlock()`; evaluation or synthetic playback invocation is not
  autoplay evidence.
- Audio assets are local bytes behind typed UUID references. Runtime handles and decoded
  buffers never enter saved data, and no remote audio fallback exists.
- Music/SFX/UI always route through their category bus and Master. Natural completion,
  explicit stop, owner deactivation, pool release, preview stop/unmount, and runtime dispose
  release voices deterministically.
- Spatial/listener pose uses current AudioParam properties; deprecated
  `setPosition()`/`setOrientation()` is absent.
- Browser automation can prove the unlocked AudioContext path, scheduled voice state, and
  cleanup. It has no acoustic sensor and therefore does not prove that a human heard the
  generated 440 Hz tone.
- M10d storage, replication, and platform packages remain absent.

  clip and injects the first real Audio Clip UUID; other programmatic callers must provide a
  valid reference.

  mixed dynamic/static import warnings, and pnpm's Node `url.parse` deprecation remain
  outside M10c. They do not fail configured gates and were not changed.
